## Overview

The [proposed RAPIDS v27.02 release schedule](/maintainers/#proposed-release-v27-02-schedule)
is being aligned with the release planning spreadsheet. Development ends on
January 18, 2027, and burn down starts on January 19, 2027, one week later than
previously proposed. The release remains scheduled for February 10–11, 2027.

## Impact

Maintainers should plan for the following proposed dates:

| Phase | Project group | Start | End | Working days |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Development | All | November 10, 2026 | January 18, 2027 | 42 |
| Burn Down | cuDF/RMM/rapids-cmake/raft/dask-cuda/KvikIO/ucxx/rapidsmpf/nvForest | January 19, 2027 | January 25, 2027 | 5 |
| Burn Down | Others | January 19, 2027 | February 1, 2027 | 10 |
| Code Freeze/Testing | cuDF/RMM/rapids-cmake/raft/dask-cuda/KvikIO/ucxx/rapidsmpf/nvForest | January 26, 2027 | February 9, 2027 | 11 |
| Code Freeze/Testing | Others | February 2, 2027 | February 9, 2027 | 6 |
| Release | All | February 10, 2027 | February 11, 2027 | 2 |

The code-freeze/testing windows are shorter to preserve the proposed release dates.
Dates remain tentative and are subject to change.
